# Understanding tiers

AI Connect has two tiers: Free and Pro.

## Free ($0/month)

- **2 integrations** maximum
- **1 project** maximum
- Connectors: WordPress + SendGrid only
- Community support

## Pro ($49/month)

- **Unlimited integrations**
- **Unlimited projects**
- All connectors: WordPress, SendGrid, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenClaw, Auth0, Stripe, GitHub
- Priority support

## Grandfathered access

If you signed up during AI Connect's early period, you have Pro access without paying. Your Billing page will show "You have grandfathered Pro access."

Converting to paid Pro is optional — it supports AI Connect's development. Click "Convert to paid Pro" on the Billing page to start a subscription.

## What happens when you hit a Free limit?

When you try to add a 3rd integration, create a 2nd project, or use a Pro-only connector as a Free user, a modal appears offering upgrade to Pro.

Downgraded Pro users keep their existing resources — they just can't create more. So if you had 5 integrations on Pro and downgrade to Free, all 5 stay accessible but you can't add a 6th.

## Upgrading

Settings → Billing → **Upgrade to Pro**. You'll be redirected to Stripe's hosted checkout page. After payment, Pro activates immediately.

See [Upgrading to Pro](#upgrading-to-pro) for the full flow.
