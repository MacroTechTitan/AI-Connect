# Upgrading to Pro

AI Connect Pro ($49/month) removes the Free tier's limits: unlimited integrations, unlimited projects, and every connector — including OpenClaw, Auth0, Stripe, and GitHub — plus Project Genesis auto-wiring and priority support.

For a side-by-side comparison of what each tier includes, see [Understanding tiers](#understanding-tiers).

## Upgrading

Settings → Billing → **Upgrade to Pro**.

You'll be redirected to Stripe's hosted checkout page. Enter your payment method, confirm, and you'll land back on AI Connect with Pro active immediately.

## Grandfathered access

Early users have Pro access with no subscription required — you'll see "You have grandfathered Pro access" on the Billing page. Converting to a paid subscription is optional; it supports AI Connect's development. You can start one from the same page whenever you like.

## What Pro unlocks

- **Unlimited integrations** — connect as many services as you need
- **Unlimited projects** — no cap on Project Genesis provisioning
- **All connectors** — WordPress, SendGrid, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenClaw, Auth0, Stripe, GitHub
- **Project Genesis auto-wiring** — Auth0 and Stripe credentials synced into new projects automatically
- **Priority support**

## Downgrading

If you cancel and drop back to Free, your existing integrations and projects are **not** deleted. You can view, edit, and delete them — you just can't create new ones beyond the Free tier limits.
